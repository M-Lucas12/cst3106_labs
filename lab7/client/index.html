🎯 Game Rules

  • Roll five dice — you have up to three rolls per turn.
  • Click a die to hold it before re-rolling.
  • After rolling, click a scoreboard category to record your score for the turn.
  • Each category may be scored only once.
  • The game ends when all categories are filled. Highest total wins.
